Fantasy Kitchen redesign

Three directions. Pick one.

Each direction has two working pages, This week (home) and The Menu (rankings), built on the sample data. Open them on your phone: that is where most of the traffic lands from X.

A. Night Service

The kitchen you already have, rebuilt like an app. Same black, amber heat and condensed type, so it still looks like the account.

  • Bottom tab bar on phones: This week, Rankings, Waivers, Start/Sit, More.
  • Tiers glow hot to cold. Every player gets a clear action tag.
  • Desktop: rankings with notes inline and a sticky side column.

B. The Ticket

Every piece is an order ticket clipped to the rail over the pass. The boldest look of the three, and the one people will recognize when a screenshot goes around X.

  • The week's schedule is the navigation: a rail of tickets, today's pulled forward.
  • Calls land like rubber stamps: START QB1, CLAIM 25-35%, PIVOT.
  • Desktop: every position is its own ticket, side by side on the rail.

C. Chef's Menu

A printed restaurant menu: cream paper, serif courses, dotted leaders from each player to his call. Calm and premium, a natural home for a paid tier later. Follows the phone's light or dark setting.

  • Each tier is a course; each player reads like a dish, with the note as its description.
  • Home is this week's menu, day by day, with what has been served.
  • Desktop: the menu opens into a two-page spread.
