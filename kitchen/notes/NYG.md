## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-08-05: The Giants used no-huddle on 20% of 2025 dropbacks (fourth highest) and Jaxson Dart got the second-largest YPA boost from it; Matt Nagy's Chiefs offenses ranked 31st in no-huddle rate the last three years. [8-5 fantasy takeaways from all 32 teams]
- 2026-08-05: Jaxson Dart has said the Giants' offense under Matt Nagy and John Harbaugh will be run-heavy; Isaiah Likely was added and the offseason drumbeat is that he will be featured as a receiver. [8-5 all upside team]
- 2026-08-17: Matt Nagy historically involves tight ends heavily in his offenses; Jameis Winston is the backup quarterback if Dart gets hurt. [8-17 Breakouts and Injury stuff]
- 2026-08-20: The Giants ran a lot of no-huddle in 2025 and Dart's metrics were among the league's best in no-huddle and among the worst otherwise; Brian Daboll was fired and the system is changing. [8-20 bust and value picks for 2026]
