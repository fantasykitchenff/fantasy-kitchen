# LAR: Los Angeles Rams

Facts the kitchen knows about this team. Seeded 2026-09-28 from the project's preseason research (June 24 to Aug 26, 2026); the kitchen's runs append below each section, newest last. Dated, backward-looking facts only. Sources in brackets are the project doc names. No analyst is named; a fact is what was reported or observed.

## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-08-04: Over three seasons with Mike LaFleur as OC the Rams threw 26, 22 and 46 passing TDs at TD rates of 4.5%, 3.9% and 7.7%; since 2011 only one team stayed above a 7% rate the next year, and it still declined. [8-4 Regression And Fantasy Draft Values]
- 2026-08-05: The Rams ranked fourth in motion rate and first in play-action rate in 2025 and carry the highest average implied total of the season. [8-5 fantasy takeaways from all 32 teams]
- 2026-08-06: The Rams lean on 13 personnel, sometimes with Davante Adams as the lone receiver; observers think Nacua should be the one on the field in those sets. [8-6 1st round picks downsides]
- 2026-08-12: The Rams expect expanded multiple-TE sets (a five-TE rotation) to create more passing opportunities for Kyren Williams, since a TE can pick up the blitz instead of Williams. [8-12 130 training camp storylines and league updates]
- 2026-08-19: Sean McVay's backfields have finished bottom-two in the league in running back targets in essentially every season he has coached the Rams. [8-19 RBs drafting and fading]
