## Coaching and play-calling

- 2026-06-24: Frank Reich, 64, is the new OC and play caller under Aaron Glenn, replacing Tanner Engstrand; he went 3-5-1 in Indianapolis in 2022 (fired), 1-10 in Carolina in 2023 (fired), sat out 2024 and 4-8 at Stanford in 2025. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
- 2026-08-05: New OC Frank Reich, 64, is the oldest coordinator in the NFL; since 2021 his offenses have had the lowest motion rate on dropbacks of any play caller. [8-5 fantasy takeaways from all 32 teams]
- 2026-08-18: Frank Reich runs the Jets' offense in 2026 with Geno Smith at quarterback; Reich's most recent NFL offense in 2023 was a static, heavy 11-personnel attack. [8-18 WR targets and avoids]
- 2026-08-19: Frank Reich's offenses have been 11-personnel, RPO-heavy with the first-read player eating targets; Geno Smith's offenses have run about 60% WR target rates with ~40% to outside receivers. [8-19 Offenses to look out for]

## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-08-06: Frank Reich has historically favored the tight end position, and there is no locked-in No. 2 pass-catcher behind Garrett Wilson and Adonai Mitchell. [8-6 Mid-Round Values And Market Score]
- 2026-08-12: OC Frank Reich's background is 11 personnel and a spread-it-around passing game; the Jets want to dabble in two-TE looks but Kenyon Sadiq's hernia (a few more weeks) affects that short term. [8-12 Camp Updates Article]
