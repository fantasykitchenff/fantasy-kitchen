## Quarterback

- 2026-08-04: Baker Mayfield, C.J. Stroud, Jaxson Dart and Bo Nix all had sub-40% deep-ball completion rates in 2025, a bucket that historically improves the following season. [8-4 Regression And Fantasy Draft Values]
- 2026-08-05: Since 2000, rookie QBs who averaged 17+ fantasy points per game declined about 6% as sophomores on average, while those under 17 improved about 24%. [8-5 year 2 players]

## Tight ends

- 2026-08-15: In 2025 the gap between TE2 and TE12 in points per game was 1.3, the tightest in six years; Fannin (undrafted), Loveland (waiver add), Pitts (after London's injury) and Goedert (red-zone role) emerged in-season. [8-15 Tips]

## Rookies

- 2026-08-15: Over the last decade, WRs drafted in the top 15 averaged 75% of snaps and 7+ targets per game in their first six weeks; round-2 rookies averaged 48% of snaps and 3.7 targets, round-3 rookies 30% and 2.1. [8-15 Tips]
