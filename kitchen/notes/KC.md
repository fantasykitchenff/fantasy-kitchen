## Coaching and play-calling

- 2026-06-24: Eric Bieniemy returns as OC replacing Matt Nagy, though Andy Reid is expected to remain the primary offensive architect; Bieniemy said the goal is to make Kenneth Walker an all-around back and get him the ball more. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
- 2026-07-29: Eric Bieniemy is back as Chiefs OC; the Chiefs finished no lower than sixth in points or yards during his 2018-22 stint and have not finished above 15th in points since he left. [7-29 RB analysis]
- 2026-08-12: Andy Reid has not used a true three-down back since Kareem Hunt's 2017 rookie year; KC has pass-catching backs Emari Demercado and Brashard Smith and drafted explosive runner Emmett Johnson. [8-12 high value touches RBs]

## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-07-14: Ranked 25th in pre-snap motion and 30th in play-action rate in 2025; Eric Bieniemy takes over the offense from Matt Nagy under Andy Reid. Favored in each of its first six games. [7-14 afc west breakdown]

- 2026-08-05: Much of the Chiefs' 2025 third-down success came from Mahomes' scrambling; adding Walker should help the goal-line offense by forcing defenses to respect the run. [8-5 ice and fire show]
- 2026-08-05: Rashee Rice: Rice's production is offense-specific: touch passes, screens, short-area targets and goal-line looks in Kansas City; he has not shown a downfield route tree. [8-5 ice and fire show]

- 2026-08-13: Kansas City finished first, second and first in pass rate over expectation the last three seasons; Matt Nagy is gone from the staff and the offense is expected to lean more on the run with Walker. [8-13 2026 man vs machine]
