## Coaching and play-calling

- 2026-06-24: Jesse Minter is the new head coach and Declan Doyle, 30, is OC and play caller after one year as a game planner under Ben Johnson in Chicago and prior years as a TE coach under Sean Payton; he has never called plays. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
- 2026-07-07: Jesse Minter, a young defensive-minded coach, replaced John Harbaugh as Ravens head coach; Declan Doyle, a protege of the Ben Johnson system, is the first-year OC after Todd Monken left for Cleveland. [7-7 afc north breakdown]
- 2026-08-05: Jesse Minter is the new Ravens head coach with Declan Doyle as OC; Baltimore led the league in early-down rush rate in 2025 while Doyle's Bears ranked 16th, implying more early-down passing. [8-5 top 10 QB rankings]
- 2026-08-07: Zay Flowers: New HC Jesse Minter said Flowers is not just a slot, outside or gadget guy and that he wants him touching the ball in as many ways as possible. [8-7 Fantasy Football League Winners]

## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-08-04: Baltimore averaged just 24.8 pass attempts per game in 2025 with the second-lowest pass rate over expected of the Lamar Jackson era; teams under 28 attempts per game since 2011 rebounded by 4.4 per game the next year. [8-4 Regression And Fantasy Draft Values]
- 2026-08-05: The Ravens ran on 55% of early downs in 2025, the NFL's highest rate; new OC Declan Doyle comes from a Bears offense that ranked 16th in neutral pass rate, and Baltimore lost center Tyler Linderbaum and several TEs. [8-5 fantasy takeaways from all 32 teams]
- 2026-08-09: Near the goal line Baltimore has leaned on Derrick Henry; Zay Flowers has rarely scored from in close, and Lamar's rushing is a club in the bag rather than a featured short-yardage weapon. [8-9 QB preview]
- 2026-08-12: Doyle's Ravens offense will use far more under-center snaps (opening boot action) and more 11 personnel than the Ricard/Likely heavy sets of the prior regime, while mixing in plenty of 12 and 13. [8-12 130 training camp storylines and league updates]
- 2026-08-14: The Ravens ranked 29th with 57.3 plays per game in 2025 after running at least 61.8 per game in each of Lamar Jackson's first six full seasons as a starter. [8-14 136 Things I Learned Doing 2026 Fantasy Football Projections]
- 2026-08-19: The Ravens are expected to run an entirely different offense under new OC Declan Doyle; rookies Matthew Hibner and Jacobe Lane emerged in the preseason as new pieces around Lamar Jackson. [8-19 RBs drafting and fading]
