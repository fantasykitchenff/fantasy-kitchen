## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-06-24: Patullo's 2025 offense leaned on hitch routes, failed to use pass catchers horizontally, lost explosiveness and gave Hurts a career-low scramble rate and the fewest designed runs per game of his career. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
