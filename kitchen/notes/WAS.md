## Coaching and play-calling

- 2026-06-24: David Blough replaces Cliff Kingsbury as OC and play caller after two years as assistant QB coach; he says the offense will be under center more, run more zone, use more play action, move McLaurin around and use Sinnott. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
- 2026-07-23: Kliff Kingsbury departed and David Blough now runs Washington's offense; the team added Chig Okonkwo (3 years, $17M), Rashaad White and third-round WR Antonio Williams and let Deebo, Ertz and Ekeler go. [7-23 nfc east breakdown]
- 2026-07-28: David Blough: First-time play caller David Blough said his first goal was getting McLaurin 10 targets a game; beat reporting expects a more balanced offense with more motion and play action than under Kliff Kingsbury. [7-28 10 ADP Debate Session]

## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-08-04: New play caller David Blough, promoted from assistant QB coach, has talked about more under-center and play-action concepts; the Commanders have the sixth-easiest fantasy RB schedule. [8-4 10 Fantasy Sleepers and Role Shifts]
- 2026-08-10: Washington is expected to use plenty of two-TE sets, with Diggs and McLaurin on the field for most snaps ahead of Antonio Williams, Treylon Burks and Luke McCaffrey. [8-10 Market Monday]
- 2026-08-13: Washington ran out of shotgun at an 89% rate over the last two seasons, by far the highest in the NFL; losing Tunsil is expected to push the offense under center more with rollouts and a more diversified run game. [8-13 Fantasy Draft Values And Injury Shifts]
