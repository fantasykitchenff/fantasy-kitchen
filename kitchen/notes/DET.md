## Coaching and play-calling

- 2026-06-24: Drew Petzing replaces John Morton as OC; Dan Campbell fired Morton and took over play-calling in Week 10 of 2025 and would likely do so again if Petzing struggles. Petzing's Cardinals were second in YPC in 2023-24. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
- 2026-07-18: Dan Campbell took over play calling during 2025 after being unhappy with how the weapons were used following Ben Johnson's departure; Aidan Hutchinson is healthy. [7-18 nfc north breakdown]
- 2026-07-28: New OC Drew Petzing dropped Brissett back ~50 times a game in Arizona and now has pocket passer Jared Goff; with a shredded secondary (Terrion Arnold loss) Detroit may have to score a lot. [7-28 Training Camp Rankings Update]
- 2026-07-28: Dan Campbell: Detroit's passing game looked similar from the Ben Johnson era through last season, with Dan Campbell the guiding force; the Petzing hire is viewed mainly as fixing a run game that was poor under John Morton. [7-28 10 ADP Debate Session]
- 2026-07-28: Drew Petzing: New Lions OC Drew Petzing ran the third-most 13-personnel plays over the last two seasons with the second-most passes from it; his and Stefanski's QBs (Brissett 34%, Murray 34%) targeted tight ends at the highest rates. [7-28 10 ADP Debate Session]
- 2026-08-03: Drew Petzing is the Lions' new offensive coordinator after running Arizona's tight-end-centric, Trey McBride-focused offense; Arizona's 2025 pass rate was inflated by garbage time and thin receiver health. [8-3 training camp week 1 winners and losers]
- 2026-08-04: Lions OC Drew Petzing dropped Jacoby Brissett back 50 times a game in Arizona last season; Goff is a similar but far better pocket passer; the Lions secondary is in shambles and they play indoors. [8-4 Training Camp News And Rankings]
- 2026-08-06: New OC Drew Petzing is viewed as hired mainly to improve the run game; Dan Campbell has a strong hand in the passing game, which looked similar to the Ben Johnson era in 2025. [8-6 1st round picks downsides]
- 2026-08-06: Petzing's Cardinals offense ranked second in the league in rushing effectiveness against two-high defenses with James Conner, a more smashmouth wrinkle. [8-6 1st round picks downsides]
- 2026-08-26: New OC Drew Petzing threw downfield aggressively with Jacoby Brissett in Arizona in 2025 and has a history of scheming for tight ends. [8-26 rankings and news updates]
