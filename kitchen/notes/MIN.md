## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-08-04: The Vikings ran 56.1 plays per game in 2025, bottom 10 in drives ending in scores and among the slowest paces; since 2011 every team at 58 or fewer plays per game increased the next year, by 4.8 per game on average. [8-4 Regression And Fantasy Draft Values]
- 2026-08-05: 2025 had the lowest pass rate over expectation and no-huddle rate of the Kevin O'Connell era; Kyler Murray's Arizona offenses used no-huddle on 24% of plays since 2019, second most in the league. [8-5 fantasy takeaways from all 32 teams]
- 2026-08-06: Kevin O'Connell overhauled the run game the day after the 2025 season ended, saying at the Combine 'I want to be a vertical wide-zone team.' New personnel groupings, bespoke motion and staple run concepts were installed. [8-6 Vikings run game revamp]
- 2026-08-06: Backs are drilled on precise tracks aimed at the outside leg of the last blocker and QBs on fast handoffs; coaches warn the scheme takes time to sync and one missed link ruins a play. [8-6 Vikings run game revamp]
- 2026-08-18: Kevin O'Connell says the lesson from 2025 is to rely on the defense and run better; the staff changed the scheme the day after the season ended to a vertical wide-zone (49ers-style) system to fit its personnel. [8-18 sleeper picks]
- 2026-08-22: Jordan Mason: Minnesota's run game is being retooled toward outside zone, the concept Mason broke out in with San Francisco; he projects as the early-down and goal-line back with Kyler Murray at quarterback. [8-22 10 players we cant stop drafting]
- 2026-08-24: Kevin O'Connell installed a vertical wide-zone run scheme this offseason modeled on San Francisco's and hired Frank Smith from Miami; he has said the team must commit more to the run game. [8-24 adp adjustments]
