# PIT: Pittsburgh Steelers

Facts the kitchen knows about this team. Seeded 2026-09-28 from the project's preseason research (June 24 to Aug 26, 2026); the kitchen's runs append below each section, newest last. Dated, backward-looking facts only. Sources in brackets are the project doc names. No analyst is named; a fact is what was reported or observed.

## Coaching and play-calling

- 2026-06-24: Mike McCarthy is the new HC and play caller after Tomlin's exit, replacing Arthur Smith's offense; his Dallas offenses were top eight in plays per game all five years and his Packers offenses lived in 3-WR shotgun sets. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
- 2026-07-07: Mike Tomlin walked away after 19 seasons without a losing record; Mike McCarthy is the new Steelers head coach and Brian Angelichio replaces Arthur Smith as offensive coordinator. [7-7 afc north breakdown]
- 2026-07-28: Mike McCarthy: Over 24 seasons calling plays, McCarthy's offenses have averaged 11th in pass attempts and 18th in rush attempts, favoring three-receiver sets and quick passing; he now runs the Steelers with Aaron Rodgers. [7-28 10 ADP Debate Session]
- 2026-08-04: Mike McCarthy now runs the Steelers' offense and signed Rico Dowdle, who played for him in Dallas. [8-4 important training camp battles]
- 2026-08-12: New DC Patrick Graham is moving T.J. Watt between both edges and using Nick Herbig as a stand-up A-gap blitzer; Brian Angelichio is the OC. [8-12 Camp Updates Article]
- 2026-08-21: Mike McCarthy is now in charge of the Steelers offense with Aaron Rodgers at QB for what looks like his final season; DK Metcalf is the lead receiver. [8-21 mayhem mock draft]

## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-07-07: The 2025 Steelers went 10-7 (7-3 in one-score games), ranked 26th in yards per game and tied for the NFL's lowest share of targets to receivers (45%). [7-7 afc north breakdown]
- 2026-07-07: Pittsburgh has the most vacated targets in the league after losing Kenneth Gainwell (team leader in receptions), Jonnu Smith and Calvin Austin. [7-7 afc north breakdown]
- 2026-08-05: The 2025 Steelers ranked 28th in plays per game and only 47% of Aaron Rodgers' attempts came with three-plus receivers on the field; Mike McCarthy's Dallas offenses used three-plus receivers a league-high 83%. [8-5 fantasy takeaways from all 32 teams]
- 2026-08-11: Pittsburgh has one of the best offensive lines, runs the ball at the goal line and schemes receptions to the backfield because Aaron Rodgers does not want to get hit. [8-11 Fav Pick in each round]
- 2026-08-12: Pittsburgh spent an install day under center in multiple-TE sets; the 2026 identity is expected to match 2025's run-heavy approach, using play-action to create explosives for Aaron Rodgers. [8-12 Camp Updates Article]

## Quarterback
