## Coaching and play-calling

- 2026-06-24: Joe Brady was promoted to head coach and is expected to keep calling plays with the same structure, including an emphasis on running the ball behind a strong line with James Cook. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
- 2026-07-28: Pete Carmichael: New Bills OC Pete Carmichael comes from the Saints tree that featured running backs on choice routes, and camp reports say Cook will be used more in the passing game; Buffalo lost its OL coach and starting left guard. [7-28 10 ADP Debate Session]
- 2026-07-29: Joe Brady's promotion to head coach after two years as OC means little change on offense; the 2025 Bills were 1st in EPA per play, scoring drive rate and points. [7-29 RB analysis]
- 2026-08-06: Joe Brady was promoted from OC to head coach, the Bills have a new offensive line coach and lost a starting guard; Jim Leonhard takes over the defense from Sean McDermott. [8-6 1st round picks downsides]
