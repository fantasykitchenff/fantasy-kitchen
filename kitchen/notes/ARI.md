## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-06-24: Arizona relied on a league-high 40 pass attempts per game with Jacoby Brissett in 2025, a volume reached only 10 times in the last decade, and faces one of the toughest schedules and the toughest division in 2026. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]
- 2026-08-04: Arizona led the league with over 38 pass attempts per game in 2025; 75% of teams at 35+ attempts in the last five years threw less the next year, though a bad defense and brutal schedule should keep them passing. [8-4 Regression And Fantasy Draft Values]
- 2026-08-05: Arizona's 2025 pass rate and play volume were extreme because it had nothing at RB and a bad defense; adding Jeremiah Love should change that. [8-5 top 10 TE rankings]
- 2026-08-13: The new offense under Mike LaFleur is expected to be more run-centric and receiver-centric and less concentrated on Trey McBride than in 2025, when McBride led the NFL in routes. [8-13 TE talk]
- 2026-08-13: Arizona is expected to lean more on the run with rookie Jeremiah Love in the backfield. [8-13 TEs]
- 2026-08-13: Arizona attempted 649 passes last year (most in the NFL) and allowed the fifth-most sacks, over 700 dropbacks; adding Jeremiyah Love (No. 3 pick), Tyler Allgeier and Carson Beck signals far fewer dropbacks. [8-13 2026 man vs machine]
- 2026-08-19: In the preseason game vs the Raiders the Cardinals' line looked cohesive and physical, with good play action off the run game and Jacoby Brissett pushing the ball downfield. [8-19 Offenses to look out for]
