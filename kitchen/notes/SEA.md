## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-08-04: Seattle had a -6.7% pass rate over expected in 2025 and ran the fewest plays in the league while trailing; teams between -5% and -8% since 2011 typically raised pass rate by about 3 points the next year. [8-4 Regression And Fantasy Draft Values]
- 2026-08-05: Seattle's 50% pass rate was the lowest in the league in 2025 and Smith-Njigba became the first player to lead the NFL in receiving yards on a bottom-three pass-rate team; the OC and run game are both new. [8-5 fantasy takeaways from all 32 teams]
- 2026-08-12: Seattle will have two tight ends on the field very often, per AJ Barner; the position facilitates the motions and shifts that are a staple of Fleury's San Francisco background. [8-12 Camp Updates Article]
- 2026-08-13: Seattle won the 2025 Super Bowl with a Walker-Charbonnet split and Kenneth Walker as Super Bowl MVP, and the staff has cited that as reason to keep splitting the backfield; Emanuel Wilson may also see work. [8-13 Fantasy Draft Values And Injury Shifts]
