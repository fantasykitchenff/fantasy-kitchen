## Coaching and play-calling

- 2026-06-24: Clint Kubiak is the new head coach and play caller, replacing the Pete Carroll/Chip Kelly regime; he was 49ers passing game coordinator, then Saints OC, then Seahawks OC, and is Gary Kubiak's son. [6-24 2026 Coaching Change Fantasy Outlook 1]

## Scheme and tendencies

- 2026-08-04: The Raiders ran 55.8 plays per game in 2025 with a bottom-10 pace and bottom-10 rates of drives ending in scores; historically such teams add roughly 80 plays the following season, many of them passes. [8-4 Regression And Fantasy Draft Values]
- 2026-08-05: The 2025 Raiders ranked dead last in EPA per rush despite using 12 personnel on 34% of plays (tied fourth highest); Clint Kubiak's 2025 Seahawks showed how to use play action from 12 personnel behind a shaky line. [8-5 fantasy takeaways from all 32 teams]
- 2026-08-05: Clint Kubiak used tight end AJ Barner as the ball carrier on Seattle's tush push last year, a possible goal-line wrinkle for Bowers or Michael Mayer in Las Vegas. [8-5 rookies to watch out for pt.2]
- 2026-08-05: Kirk Cousins: Cousins does not scramble and plays at a high pace, so Brock Bowers' target volume is expected to be higher with him than with Mendoza; Cousins was under center when Kyle Pitts broke out late last year. [8-5 rookies to watch out for pt.2]
